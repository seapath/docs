# Prerequisites

This section describes the hardware and software prerequisites to install and manage a SEAPATH infrastructure.

It also gives recommendations for machines to easily kick-start SEAPATH

> [!IMPORTANT]
> If your setup is only for testing purposes, many of the requirements described below can be worked around with specific software solutions. If you do not have the necessary equipment, do not hesitate to contact the community on Slack or on the mailing list and describe your specific needs
