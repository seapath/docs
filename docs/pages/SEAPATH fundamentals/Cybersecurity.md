# Cybersecurity

SEAPATH follows the applicable cybersecurity guidelines defined by the ANSSI in the [ANSSI-BP-028v2.0](https://cyber.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/document/linux_configuration-en-v2.pdf) document. Several mechanisms have been considered to guarantee the system security:

- System hardening
- Disk encryption
- Secrets storage and protection
- Process isolation
- Privileges management policy
- Connection encryption

On the next page is the list of the ANSSI-BP-028 recommendations along with their status on SEAPATH and an explanation of the work done or to be done.
